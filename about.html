About

Khashayar Pourtaheri

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Washington, advised by Alan Griffith. I am currently on the job market.

My research is in applied microeconomics, causal inference, and policy evaluation. I study questions spanning health, labor, and crime policy, alongside experimental research on whether LLM-based synthetic personas can reproduce human economic decision-making.

I bring rigorous analytical methods to both independent research and collaborative policy projects. My work draws on longitudinal, administrative, survey, and consumer data, using reproducible workflows in R, Python, and Stata.
